**As a** trader opening FinAlly, **I want to** see live simulated prices streaming into a dark terminal-style watchlist at http://localhost:8000, **so that** I can watch the market move in real time from a single origin.

Output: Grid shell and components under `frontend/src/components/`, `frontend/src/lib/format.ts`, flash/pulse CSS in `globals.css`.
</objective>
